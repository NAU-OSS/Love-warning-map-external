## Rationale for Choosing MIT License
After evaluating licensing options via [choosealicense.com](https://choosealicense.com/) and standard open-source guidelines, I selected the **MIT License** for the following reasons:

1. **Permissive & Unrestrictive**: The MIT License allows developers and community members to freely use, modify, distribute, and integrate this software into both open-source and commercial projects with minimal conditions.
2. **Simplicity & Clarity**: As a lightweight 3D web visualization tool, the code benefits from a straightforward license that reduces legal overhead and encourages open collaboration.
3. **Liability Protection**: It includes standard legal protection stating that the software is provided "as is" without warranty, protecting maintainers from potential liabilities.
